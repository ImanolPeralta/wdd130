Wild Current Rafting

Our mission is to provide safe and exciting rafting experiences while promoting teamwork, respect, and environmental responsibility. We believe every adventure is an opportunity to connect with nature, build friendships, and create lasting memories.

History

Founded in 2012 by two friends passionate about outdoor adventures, Wild Current Rafting has grown from a small rafting business into a company dedicated to sharing the excitement of white water rafting and creating unforgettable experiences in nature.
